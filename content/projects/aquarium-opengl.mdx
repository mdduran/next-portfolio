An aquarium with three different types of fish that separated into schools based on color. When the fish collide with one another, depending on which fish collides, will change the color of the fish.

The fish are animated with a technique called behavioral animation where the fish are considered boids. The fish have their own respective flocks, or schools (since we are talking about fish) and move together using characteristics such as cohesion, separation and alignment.
