This project consists of a playable game called Sixes Wild, and an accompanying Sixes Wild Level Builder.

The goal of Sixes Wild is to create selections of tiles that add to a sum of six (6). Tiles have number values that represent how much they contribute towards a sum of six during a move, as well as a multiplier to increase the move's score.

The level builder is used to create playable levels with tiles that can be added into the game.
