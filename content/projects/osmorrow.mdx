An adventure set on the planet of Osmorrow, where the Ogre clan has run rampant. Your mission is to help restore peace and order to this world as you liberate Osmorrow from the Ogre oppression.

Use high skilled melee and ranged combat along with swift and precise movement to quell the Ogre threat.
