Rock Lives is a survival game in which the player must defeat a horde of monsters while traveling through a rocky pass. The world is made of an array of monsters, items, and environment tiles.

The goal of this game is to survive as long as possible until the player is defeated in battle by one of the monsters or starves to death. Along the way, the player will be able to explore to find new weapons, armor and food to keep yourself alive.
